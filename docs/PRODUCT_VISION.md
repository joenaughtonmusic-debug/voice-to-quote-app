# Talk to Quote Product Vision

## Vision

Talk to Quote helps trade businesses quote faster, more consistently, and with less admin by turning real-world site conversations into accurate, editable quote drafts.

## Product Principles

- Voice-first capture for field work
- Human review before sending
- Trade-specific extraction without business-specific hardcoding
- Business knowledge comes from templates, price lists, plant libraries, and user settings
- Clear confidence warnings where information is missing or uncertain

## Target Experience

The user should be able to record a job walkthrough, add quick notes, and receive a structured draft that already understands:

- Customer and site details
- Scope of work
- Internal notes and cautions
- Missing information
- Template fit
- JMS line items
- Planting calculations where relevant

## Differentiators

- Built for trades, not generic note-taking
- Learns from uploaded business documents
- Supports item-library matching from job management systems
- Preserves raw transcript while improving extracted quote output

## Future Opportunities

- Address validation and autocomplete
- Supplier pricing sync
- Quote export to JMS platforms
- Quote approval workflows
- Team and business account management
